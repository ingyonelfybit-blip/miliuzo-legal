Privacy Policy

What we collect, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how to make us delete it.

The short version

We collect what the marketplace needs to work: who you are, where you are, and what you asked for. When a contractor pays the lead fee for a job, we give them that homeowner's name, phone number and email — that exchange is the whole product, and posting a job is your consent to it.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. There is no advertising network in this app.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your state and, if you choose one, your city
  • Your password, stored only as a salted hash — we never see it

If you are a contractor

  • Your business name and, optionally, a licence number and short bio
  • The trades you work in and the states you cover
  • A Stripe customer record, so a card can be kept on file for lead fees

As you use the app

  • Jobs you post, including title, description, budget and timeline
  • Leads you take, and the fee charged for each
  • Ratings and comments you leave
  • Which leads you opened, and which notifications you tapped
  • Your device notification token, so we can reach your phone
We do not use your device GPS. Location is the state and city you pick from a list. We never ask for your camera, contacts, or photos except when you choose to scan a card in the payment sheet, which happens entirely inside Stripe's software. Card numbers never reach Miliuzo — payment details go directly from your device to Stripe.

Why we collect it

  • To show contractors jobs in their trades and service area
  • To introduce a contractor to a homeowner once the lead fee is paid
  • To charge lead fees and send receipts
  • To send notifications you asked for
  • To detect fraud and keep the marketplace safe
  • To understand what works in the product and improve it
  • To meet legal and tax obligations

Who else sees it

Other users, but only as the marketplace requires: a contractor who pays the lead fee for your job receives your name, phone and email. Your contact details are never shown before that.

Service providers, who are contractually required to protect your data to the same standard set out here and may only use it to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage (United States)
  • Stripe — payment processing and stored cards
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications
  • Resend — sending transactional email such as receipts
  • Apple and Google — app distribution and crash diagnostics

We also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users. We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined by California law.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile: until you delete your account
  • Jobs you post: until you delete them, or your account
  • Records of lead fees charged: seven years, for tax and accounting law, with your name removed once your account is deleted
  • Ratings and comments: kept without your name, so deleting an account cannot erase honest reviews of a contractor
  • Notification tokens: removed when you sign out or delete the account
  • Support email: two years

Deleting your account

Open Account → Delete account in the app and type DELETE. This is permanent and we cannot restore it. You do not have to email anyone or wait for us to act.

Deleting removes your profile, your contact details, your saved card, your device from our notification list, jobs nobody has taken, your blocks and your reports. What remains is the financial record of lead fees charged, with your identity removed, because US tax rules require us to keep the transaction — and ratings, with your name removed.

Withdrawing consent

  • Notifications: Account → Notifications. Marketing is off unless you turn it on, and turning it off stops it at the source, not just on your phone.
  • Sharing your contact with contractors: this is inherent to posting a job. Close or delete a job to stop new contractors receiving it. Contractors who already paid keep what they paid for.
  • Everything else: delete your account.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you the personal information we hold, correct it, delete it, or send you a copy. Write to support@miliuzo.com and we will respond within 30 days.

If you live in California, the CCPA as amended by the CPRA gives you the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing — we do neither — and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of them. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

Children

Miliuzo is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and if we learn that we have, we delete it. If you believe a child has given us information, write to support@miliuzo.com.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your session is stored in the device keychain or keystore, and you can require Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode to open the app from Account → app lock. Access to production data is limited to the people who need it.

No system is perfect. If we ever suffer a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes and contact

If we change how we handle your data in a material way, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. Questions, requests, or complaints: support@miliuzo.com.
