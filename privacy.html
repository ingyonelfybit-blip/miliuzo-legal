Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Miliuzo ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our platform. By using Miliuzo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business information
  • Login credentials

Service usage information

  • Lead submissions and responses
  • Service preferences
  • Communication history
  • Transaction records

Technical information

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Usage patterns and preferences
What we do not collect. The Miliuzo mobile app does not access your device's GPS location, photos, contacts, or microphone. The service area you choose is a state and city you select yourself. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we use no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Facilitate connections between clients and contractors
  • Process and manage lead submissions
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Send important notifications about our services
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security
  • Comply with legal obligations

Information sharing

We share your information with:

  • Other users as necessary for lead processing (with your consent)
  • Service providers who assist in operating our platform
  • Legal authorities when required by law

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How lead purchases work

When a contractor purchases a lead, the client's name, phone number and email address are released to that contractor so they can be contacted about the job. Clients who post a job consent to their contact details being shared this way with the contractors who purchase their lead. Contact details are never shown before a purchase.

Service providers we use

  • Stripe — processes card payments. Card details are entered directly into Stripe's secure payment interface and are never stored on our servers.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers push notifications. This uses a device token tied to your account, which you can revoke by turning off notifications.
  • Resend — sends transactional email, such as confirmation codes and purchase receipts.

Data retention and security

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, access is restricted by row-level security rules, and session credentials are stored encrypted on your device using the platform keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Export your data

Children's privacy

Miliuzo is intended for adults contracting or providing professional services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or want to access, correct or delete your data, contact us at support@miliuzo.com.
